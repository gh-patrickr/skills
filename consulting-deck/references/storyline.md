# The 12 rules

1. Draft the governing thought first: one sentence, ≤25 words, recommendation, number, horizon.
2. Put it on slide 2 as the `summary` title and repeat it on the closing slide.
3. Make every non-divider title a declarative sentence with a finite verb and no full stop.
4. Carry the decision number in the title whenever the exhibit is quantitative.
5. Cap titles at 15 words and 90 characters; a third line means split the slide.
6. Give each slide one message; only `summary`, KPI and appendix carry more.
7. Chain adjacent titles with therefore, because, however or meanwhile — "also" is a hole.
8. Make every divider a numbered claim the section proves, never a label.
9. Hold 2–4 MECE key lines and give each at least two evidence pages; one-page sections belong inline, not behind a divider.
10. Show one exhibit per slide, at most three blocks, four bullets of ≤25 words.
11. Source every `chart`, `table` and `kpi` block in the footer.
12. Read the titles-only list before layout, then close on an ask with owner and date.

Three of the twelve fail hardest: labels, compounds joined by "and", digits missing above a numeric exhibit.

## Action titles

`[driver] + [active verb] + [quantified object] + [time or implication]`

Six patterns cover every legitimate title:

- **P1 Finding** — *Churn, not price, causes 60% of revenue leakage*
- **P2 Recommendation** — *Exiting France by Q2 2027 ends €8M of losses*
- **P3 Causal** — *Premium mix added 240bps, offsetting freight inflation*
- **P4 Conditional** — *A six-month integration slip cuts IRR to 9%*
- **P5 Comparative** — *We win 4 of 6 criteria but lose on total cost*
- **P6 Range** — *Automation saves €6–9M depending on adoption*

Twelve rewrites:

| Failure | Weak | Rewritten |
|---|---|---|
| Label | Market overview | 70% of the German market is closed to outsiders |
| Label | Next steps | Three decisions unlock a Q1 pilot in Poland |
| Process | We interviewed 40 customers | Customers defect over onboarding, not roadmap gaps |
| Hedge | Potentially attractive in the Nordics | The Nordics clears our 15% hurdle by 4 points |
| Question | Should we enter Poland? | Entering Poland through enterprise adds €40M by 2027 |
| Passive | It is recommended that spend is cut | Cutting indirect spend 15% releases €41M |
| Compound | Growth comes from pricing and new segments | Pricing, not new segments, delivers 60% of €21M |
| No number | Churn is a significant problem | Churn costs €18M a year, 60% in month one |
| No number | Synergies are substantial | Synergies reach €50M, half in procurement |
| Jargon | Leveraging digital to drive value | Automating invoice matching frees 22 FTE |
| Vague | Improve the operating model | Collapsing one layer removes 9% of overhead |
| Mismatch | Margins are improving | Volume, not price, explains the 8% gain |

Three tests:

**So-what.** Append "so what?". *Freight costs are rising* fails; *we lose 4 points of margin* passes.

**3-second.** A stranger states the claim and the number from the title alone.

**Title-only chain.** Titles one per line, exhibits removed: adjacent pairs must take a connector, no two share a stem, a partner reconstructs the argument.

**Furniture.** Kicker ≤24 characters, capitals, never the title's opening words; takeaway ≤20 words of mechanism.

## Deck skeletons

One stub per core slide; `§` expands to 2–3 evidence pages. The counts are ceilings, not targets: a deck that hits them without cutting is usually still 20% too long. No agenda page under 15 pages.

**Market entry — 15 slides (ceiling 18)**

- Enter Germany through enterprise to add €40M
- §1 Germany grows 6% a year to €2.4B
- Only 3 of 11 segments clear our hurdle
- §2 Enterprise buyers defect over onboarding, not price
- §3 Two incumbents hold 60% of the top tier
- Approve €25M and a country manager by March

**Growth strategy — 16 slides (ceiling 20)**

- Growth of €60M splits 45/35/20 market, share, M&A
- §1 Two of five segments can fund the plan
- §2 We win 4 of 6 criteria but lose on cost
- The premium tier adds €18M at 34% margin
- §3 Pricing alone recovers 3 points of margin
- Approve €14M for pricing by June

**Commercial due diligence — 21 slides (ceiling 25)**

- The target is worth €180M, 25% below the ask
- §1 The market grows 4%, half the modelled rate
- §2 Share sits in two accounts renewing in 2027
- Churn runs at 14%, twice the peer median
- §3 Revenue quality does not support 3.5×
- §4 Two red flags put €30M at risk

**Cost transformation — 15 slides (ceiling 18)**

- Indirect spend runs 22% above peer median
- §1 Four levers close €18M within 18 months
- Two levers need works-council approval before Q2
- §2 Spans of control sit 40% below peers
- §3 Wave 1 protects €6M in year one
- Approve €4M for wave 1 in January

**Board update — 8 slides (ceiling 10)**

- Cash beat plan by €4M, revenue missed by 6%
- Churn explains 70% of the revenue gap
- Runway is 14 months at the current burn
- Approve the €3M pricing programme

**Fundraising — 11 slides (ceiling 13)**

- We are raising €12M to reach €30M ARR
- §1 Finance teams lose 9 hours a week reconciling
- §2 The market reaches €1.8B by 2029
- §3 We grow 8% a month at 82% retention
- The plan reaches break-even in 26 months

## Pruning (do this twice)

The first draft is always 20–30% too long, and every page you keep costs the audience attention. Run three passes before you build:

1. **Delete test.** Remove the page. Does any later page stop making sense? If not, it was decoration — leave it out.
2. **Merge test.** Two pages making the same point with different exhibits become one page with the better exhibit; the other becomes a callout or an appendix.
3. **Divider test.** A divider costs a full page. If the section it opens runs fewer than three pages, delete the divider and let the kicker do the navigation. Dividers only pay for themselves above ~15 pages.

Budgets by deck type (body pages, excluding cover, dividers and end): board update 5–7, strategy review 8–11, market entry or cost programme 10–13, CDD 13–18, fundraising 8–11. Above the band, you have not finished cutting. State each page's `purpose:` in the spec — if you cannot finish the sentence "this page exists so the reader can…", delete it.

## Choosing a layout

Slots take only `full`, `left`+`right`, `top`+`bottom`, `top`+`left`+`right`, `top`+`col1..3`, `col1..3`, `left`+`right`+`bottom`; a fourth block is a second slide.

| Archetype | Layout, slots, block | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Cover | `cover` | ≤8 words, title ≤60 chars |
| Executive summary | `summary`, 3–4 `messages` (lead + evidence) + `banner` | 140–165 |
| Section divider | `section`, number, label, claim | ≤20 |
| Situation / context | `content`, `top`+`col1..3`, `bullets` | ≤120 |
| Key message | `content`, `left`+`right`, `bullets`+`chart` | ≤120 |
| 2×2 matrix | `content`, `full`, `matrix` | ≤80, 4 quadrants |
| Waterfall / bridge | `content`, `full`, `chart` | ≤60, 5–6 drivers |
| Value chain / process | `content`, `full`, `flow` or `steps` | ≤100, 5–7 stages |
| Roadmap | `content`, `full`, `timeline` | ≤90, 3 horizons |
| Org design | `content`, `full`, `org` | ≤80, ≤4 layers |
| Comparison table | `content`, `full`, `table` | ≤150, 4–6 criteria |
| Chart exhibit | `content`, `left`+`right`, `chart`+`callout` | ≤40, callout ≤12 |
| KPI dashboard | `content`, `full`, `kpi` | 8–12 KPIs, ≤120 |
| Risk register | `content`, `top`+`bottom`, `table`+`callout` | ≤150 |
| Recommendation | `content`, `col1..3`, `cards` | ≤165 |
| Next steps | `content`, `full`, `table` | ≤80, ≤6 rows |
| Appendix | `content`, `A1` kicker | ≤170 |
| Closing | `end` | ≤40, ask + owner + date |

`agenda` only after the summary, over 20 pages. `funnel` for sizing, `quote` one customer voice, `banner` a verdict, `text` for S and C. `qa.py` warns past 170 words or 95 title characters and errors past 118.

## Grading yourself before building

**Title score (0–100).** Assertion 25, specificity 20, brevity 15, active voice 10, single claim 10, insight 10, consistency 10. Deduct 20 banned first word, 15 question mark, 3 per excess word (max 10), 5 hedge, 25 title–exhibit mismatch. Bands 85+ pass, 70–84 revise, <70 rewrite; gate mean 85+, minimum 70.

**Storyline score (0–100).** Chain 30, vertical Q&A 25, MECE 20, summary coverage 15, balance 10. Bands 85+ build, 75–84 review, <75 restructure.

Four blocking checks:

1. **Link words.** "A ___ B": exactly one of therefore, because, however, meanwhile must fit; if only "also" fits, cut or merge.
2. **Banned words.** First word: overview, analysis, update, status, discussion, approach, context, learnings, agenda, next steps, executive summary. Anywhere: may, could, potentially, seems, likely, we believe, we analysed, we reviewed.
3. **One message.** One finite verb, one number family, no " and " or semicolon joining verbs; except `summary`, `kpi`, appendix.
4. **Summary coverage.** First four content words of every `section` title must appear in a `summary` lead or its evidence line — 100%.

Also reject duplicate stems, a `chart`/`table`/`kpi` slide without `source`, and a closing slide that misses the ask.

## Language

**Verbs.** cut, lift, shift, hold, win, lose, exit, fund, defer, remove, add, offset, clear, breach, halve, protect; delete leverage, drive, enable, facilitate, support, optimise.

**Hedges.** Delete may, might, could, potentially, possibly, seems, likely, broadly, largely, significant, substantial, up to, we believe. A range beats a hedge: *€6–9M*, not *up to €9M*.

**Numbers.** % for share, pp for a change in a rate: 2pp to 24%, never 2%; bps below one point: 240bps, not 2.4pp; symbol first, no space, one unit per deck (€25M, $1.2B); ranges with one unit (€6–9M, 15–20%); one decimal; signed deltas (−€8M); denominators where scale matters (€/tonne).

**Tense.** Present for findings, present plus "by [date]" for implications (*cuts cost 11% by 2027*), past for booked results; no "would" or "should".

**Caps.** Title ≤15 words/90 characters; takeaway ≤20/95; bullet ≤25 words, two lines; sentence 25 average, 35 max; slide ≤170 words; numerals always.

## Worked example

1. Indirect spend runs 22% above peer median
2. [therefore] Four levers close €18M within 18 months
3. [because] Procurement consolidation delivers €9M of the gap
4. [however] Two levers need works-council approval before Q2
5. [therefore] A phased wave protects €6M in year one
6. [ask] Approve €4M to start wave 1 in January
